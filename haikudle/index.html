    How to play

    Every day there's a new draw of 18 word magnets. Build a haiku from them: three lines of 5, 7 and 5 syllables. The dots on each magnet show its syllables.

    • lantern•• Today's words. Each can be used once.
    • the Glue words. Use up to 7 in your haiku.

    Tap a magnet to add it to the highlighted line, and tap a line to switch to it. Tap a word in your haiku to take it back off.

    You get one haiku a day. Once it's posted you can read everyone else's and like as many as you want.

    The day changes at 12:00 UTC (4am Pacific, 7am Eastern in winter). Liking closes then, and the top 3 are shown the next day. Ties go to whoever posted first.

    Keyboard: 1 2 3 pick a line, Backspace removes the last word, Enter posts.

    Report this haiku?

    It'll be sent to me to look at. Nothing is hidden automatically.

    About Haikudle

    Haikudle is a free daily word game. Everyone gets the same 18 words each day and writes one haiku with them, then reads and likes everyone else's. Yesterday's three most-liked haiku are shown at the top.
