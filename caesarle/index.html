Caesarle

Final answer · one shot, ends the game

How to play

Today's secret word has been run through a Caesar cipher: every letter pushed forward the same number of places (the shift, 1–25), wrapping Z back to A. With a shift of 1, PASTE becomes QBTUF.

Probe rows (up to 6). Each one is a 5-letter guess, plus an optional shift guess. Leave the shift blank to skip the check. Every guess is checked against the encrypted word:

  • T this letter is in the cipher, right spot
  • B in the cipher, wrong spot
  • M not in the cipher
  • 7 ↑ the real shift is higher
  • 20 ↓ the real shift is lower
  • 13 ✓ you've cracked the shift

A probe can be a real word (SLATE) or a real word written in today's cipher (TMBUF, if the shift is 1). Cipher gibberish in the wrong shift is rejected, and so is anything else that isn't a word. A rejected probe doesn't use a row, but it costs 5 points.

Final answer. Tap the row under the board (or press Tab) and type the real, decoded word. You only get one try, and it ends the game.

About Caesarle

Caesarle is a free daily word game, like Wordle with a cipher twist. Each day's five-letter word is encrypted with a Caesar cipher, the code Julius Caesar used, where every letter shifts the same number of places along the alphabet. Probe the hidden word with real words or with words written in the cipher, use the arrows to find the shift, then lock in your answer. A new puzzle unlocks every day at midnight.
