Rooted

Beta · The dictionary is still growing. When a real word gets rejected, it's logged anonymously so it can be added.

How to play

Connect two words by growing roots from both ends until they meet.

  1. Tap a part of a word on either side, like norm in ab·norm·al.
  2. Type any word with that same part: enormous. It sprouts underneath.
  3. Keep growing from either side. When a word you type is already on the other side, the roots meet and you win.

Kinds of links

  • Root: the same root. norm → norm
  • Relative: a historically related root, like Latin ped and Greek pod (both "foot"), or ex- and extra-.
  • Prefix or suffix: ex-, re-, -al… Each one works once per puzzle.

Watch for false friends: the ped in encyclopedia means "child," not "foot," and terrify has nothing to do with terra.

Every word you add stays on the board, so you can branch from any of them.

Par is the shortest chain using roots alone. Prefixes and suffixes are allowed and can get you under par, but they cost points.

Points

Your chain is the words that actually connect the two sides. Words left on dead-end branches are extra: exploring is cheap, a short chain is what counts.

Connect the two words+100
Each link in your chain−10
Each prefix or suffix link in your chain (⬜)−15 more
Each extra word−5
🌳 Pure roots: no prefix or suffix links in your chain+60

Par is the shortest chain using roots alone, so the most you can score is a pure chain at par with no extra words: 120 on a par-4 puzzle, 130 on par 3. A pure-roots chain beats any shortcut, even a few links over par.
